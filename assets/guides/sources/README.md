# Guides hero sources

The two PNG masters in this directory were generated for Thai-Land.co.il with the built-in image generator on 2026-08-10. They are project-bound source assets and are not intended for the production plugin package.

`scripts/build_guide_assets.py` binds their exact hashes and dimensions and produces the responsive WebP files used by the Guides runtime.

## visas-entry-thailand-v1-master.png

Purpose: entry, visa, TDAC, and permanent-residence guides.

Prompt direction: premium natural editorial photography in a modern Thai airport, with a blank travel booklet, a smartphone showing only abstract non-readable arrival-form fields, tropical greenery, a low believable Bangkok skyline in humid haze, an unbranded aircraft, and calm negative space for Hebrew HTML copy. No QR codes, barcodes, logos, flags, readable text, generated lettering, or fake landmarks.

## cannabis-law-thailand-v1-master.png

Purpose: current cannabis law guide.

Prompt direction: restrained medical and legal editorial still life with one cannabis leaf, a blank medical jar and prescription card, a brass balance, a travel pouch, Thai teal and warm brass colors, calm negative space for Hebrew HTML copy, and no smoke, recreational cues, logos, readable text, or generated lettering.
