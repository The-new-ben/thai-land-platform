This is an evidence snapshot for content ownership and product decisions. It
does not set public claims and it does not treat search-result wording as a
verified fact.

## Current result groups

### Broad travel planning

- [Go Thailand](https://www.go-thailand.co.il/) covers destinations, hotels,
  flights, food, weather, routes, attractions, and trip organization. Its
  visible strength is breadth inside travel.
- [Lametayel Thailand](https://www.lametayel-thailand.com/planning-a-trip-to-thailand/)
  owns a clear planning intent with destinations, transport, weather, and
  frequently asked questions in one guide.
- [Rak Thailand](https://rakthailand.co.il/) is service-led. The page centers
  personal trip planning and human support rather than a national data tree.

Decision: Thai-Land should not create many interchangeable general travel
guides. One national travel owner leads to region, province, city, island,
season, itinerary, transport, accommodation, and attraction owners. Each page
answers one main task and links sideways only where the task truly continues.

### Relocation and long stays

- [Walla relocation guide](https://travel.walla.co.il/item/3744039) combines
  lifestyle reasons, living costs, popular areas, language, and Israeli tax
  considerations.
- [Greenberg relocation guide](https://www.greenbergm.co.il/relocation-to-thailand/)
  concentrates on finance, tax, legal status, first-month costs, deposits,
  rent, insurance, and cash flow.

Decision: living pages need local monthly budgets and neighborhood choices,
while visa, work, company, tax, health, school, and insurance questions retain
separate owners and stricter freshness. A destination page may summarize those
questions but must not compete for their main search intent.

### Real estate

- [ThaiProperty](https://thaiproperty.co.il/) exposes project cards, update
  dates, price per square meter, unit types, and project facilities.
- [Anglo Saxon Thailand FAQ](https://www.anglosaxonthailand.com/faq/) answers
  transaction questions and agency fees for Israeli buyers.

Decision: a useful property result needs more than project copy. The target
record contains project identity, developer, coordinates, construction and
handover dates, tenure, unit inventory, sizes, asking-price range, price per
square meter, common fees, sinking fund, estimated rent, operating costs,
vacancy assumption, comparable listings, update date, and change history.
Estimated figures must be labelled with date and assumptions. Marketing yield
and calculated yield remain separate fields.

### Commerce and community infrastructure

- [BuyInThai](https://buyinthai.com/he/) combines Thai brands, categories, and
  shipping from Thailand to Israel.
- [Chabad Thailand](https://chabadthailand.co.il/) shows that the Israeli and
  Jewish service network is location-based, with activity in Bangkok, Phuket,
  Chiang Mai, Koh Samui, Pai, and Koh Phangan.

Decision: the marketplace needs geography as a first-class relation. A product
can ship to several areas, a provider can serve several areas, and a physical
branch can belong to one place while the organization belongs to many. These
are typed relations and must not be forced into the primary breadcrumb tree.

### Homepage community facts used

- [Chabad Thailand](https://chabadthailand.co.il/) lists active locations in
  Bangkok, central Bangkok, Phuket, Koh Samui, Chiang Mai, Koh Phangan, and Pai.
- Its [restaurant directory](https://chabadthailand.co.il/restaurants/) lists
  kosher restaurants, deliveries, and groceries by location.
- The Phuket, Koh Samui, and Chiang Mai branch pages provide the local details
  summarized in the homepage cards. Opening hours and telephone numbers were
  intentionally left out of the homepage because they need a dated local
  record and a freshness owner before publication.

## Own-result issue found

The search result for [thai-land.co.il](https://thai-land.co.il/) still showed
an older title and snippet containing broad service claims and old descriptive
text. The live homepage has already been replaced with direct language, but
search recrawling has not caught up with the new release.

Actions:

1. Keep the live title, description, canonical, schema, and visible H1 aligned.
2. Remove or rewrite old indexed copy that contains unsupported service claims
   or time-sensitive statements without dates.
3. Submit the canonical homepage and sitemap for recrawling after the next
   content release.
4. Track the returned title and snippet rather than assuming Google will use
   the submitted wording.

## Initial intent ownership

The first national owners are:

1. Thailand overview and entry point
2. destinations in Thailand
3. Thailand map
4. tourism and trip planning
5. living in Thailand
6. real estate in Thailand
7. business in Thailand
8. services in Thailand
9. Israelis in Thailand
10. Thailand marketplace

Every province, city, island, neighborhood, project, attraction, provider, and
product must attach to one of those owners and to a canonical geography entity.
Filters, map state, search results, sort orders, and comparison parameters are
not independent indexable pages.

## Architecture pattern worth carrying forward

[Japan Guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e623a.html) separates regions,
prefectures, destinations, interests, planning, transport, accommodation, and
living. [Visit Norway](https://www.visitnorway.com/places-to-go/) separates
main regions from cities and local recommendations. The useful pattern is not
their visual style. It is the ability to reach a place through geography and
reach the same place through a task without creating two canonical owners for
the same intent.
